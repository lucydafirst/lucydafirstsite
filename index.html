01 / ABOUT

Lucy First — formerly known as Lucydafirst — is a Canadian DJ, producer and audio creative with over a decade of experience behind the decks and on stage.

Rooted in hip-hop and old-school breaks, her sound pushes into electro, heavy low end and forward-thinking electronic textures. Her approach is built around movement, detail and personality: music with grit, bounce and a point of view.

From club nights and festival stages to sound work and independent releases, Lucy brings a hands-on understanding of music, performance and the culture around it.
